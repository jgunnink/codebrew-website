Use this template as a starting point for anything you want to share: a conference talk, a new open-source tool, a Google Cloud architecture pattern, or a business milestone.

Entries do not need to be long essays. Two or three crisp paragraphs with the key context and a link to the slides, recording, or repository is plenty.

## Key takeaways

- **Context:** What problem or topic was addressed, and why it matters to engineering leaders.
- **Architecture:** The practical Google Cloud or software engineering trade-offs involved.
- **Outcome:** What teams can take away and apply directly.

> Real architecture comes from running real systems in production, not slide decks.

To create a new entry from the CLI, run:

```bash
hugo new content/news/my-new-update.md
```
